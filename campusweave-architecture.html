REVIEW QUESTIONS

Challenge the boundaries, not the colours.

  • Does every actor have a clear identity, tenant and purpose?
  • Can any payment callback, vendor or AI path bypass a business gate?
  • Which records are canonical, and who owns corrections?
  • Which provider outage can stop admission, payment or notification?
  • What must be synchronous, asynchronous, auditable or recoverable?

HOW TO READ

Top to bottom is the control flow.

Experience: actor-specific surfaces only.
Access: authentication is separate from authorization and tenant context.
Domains: business rules and state transitions.
Runtime: data, queues, search, audit and recovery.
Providers: replaceable adapters, never the system of record.
