Our story

A recipe passed down, not reinvented

Guru Sweet Mart is run by the descendants of Kakasura Madappa, the Mysore Palace cook traditionally credited with creating Mysore Pak. The recipe has travelled through generations of the same family — the same proportions of gram flour, sugar and ghee, cooked the same patient way.
