Quiet to loud

Two agents. One calm voice on the radio. A heist that keeps getting louder.

Exo and Eve wake in a stranger’s apartment with no memory of who they were. A voice on the radio sends them into DayCorp’s buildings to bring out the evidence a case needs: one goes in, the other keeps watch on the radio. Do the work, and maybe you get your past back.

Bring it home INTACT.

Every level you finish can be played again at three difficulties. Your best grade and time are kept, and the end of each level tells you what the next grade up needs.

Being seen is not the end

An alarm, not a restart.

Getting spotted raises the alarm. The guards who saw you open fire, the rest close in on where you were, and backup arrives up the stairs, by the lift, down a rope or by helicopter.

Break their line of sight and they search, then stand down, but the building stays on edge. Slip away unseen, or fight your way out. If you’d rather be sent back when you’re seen, switch on Classic.
