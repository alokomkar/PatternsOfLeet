Concept 2 of 5

Begin with the job the answer must do.

A support answer is not good because it sounds polished. It is good when it resolves the user’s need accurately, safely, and with the right next action.

Try it

A user asks: “Can I export all account data before deleting my workspace?” Which criteria matter most?

Exactly. Those criteria reflect whether the answer safely completes the user’s job—not merely how it sounds.
