The real problem

Every planning technique breaks the moment you have more than one goal.

Without Longview

Plans compete for hours no one is tracking.

A startup, a fitness goal, a house project — each lives in its own app or notebook.

  • Nothing accounts for shared weekly capacity across goals.
  • Generic AI suggestions ignore your other commitments.
  • A missed day quietly cascades with no record of why.
  • Recommendations arrive as prose you have to trust blindly.
With Longview

One shared portfolio, one reviewable step at a time.

Every Plan declares its weekly hours and working days against one finite budget.

  • Clara reasons only from your full committed capacity.
  • Every proposed schedule change is a preview, never a silent edit.
  • Completions, approvals, and rationale stay in an audit trail.
  • Structured, schema-validated output — not free-form chat text.

Core system

Built around the parts that break when done naively.

01 · BOUNDED RECOMMENDATIONS

Clara sees one Plan, not your whole history.

Each request carries only the selected Plan and Today's step, and returns a strict schema-validated payload, never free text.

02 · EXPLICIT APPROVAL

Every change previews before it happens.

A proposed schedule change shows before/after, expected version, and downstream effect before one idempotent write occurs.

03 · CAPACITY-AWARE CALENDAR

The day's plan is a proposal, not a mandate.

Retry, replacement, and break carryover are handled explicitly inside your real time window — never silently.

04 · ATTRIBUTED RESEARCH

External evidence arrives with a real source.

Grounded findings show up as a reviewable card with a source link and retrieval time — never merged into a Plan automatically.

05 · VERSIONED PLAN BRIEF

Accepted research becomes durable memory.

Promoted findings create an immutable, versioned Brief with a current pointer you can trace back through.

06 · OFFLINE-CAPABLE PWA

Keeps working when the connection doesn't.

Installable shell, offline fallback, and a durable local outbox for Today completions made while disconnected.

The model proposes. You dispose.

Clara's output stays advisory until a human reviews it.

Authorization, current-version checks, and one idempotent Firestore transaction stand between any suggestion and your actual Plan.
