Guru Sweet Mart · Phase 1

Estimated Running Costs

As of 14 November 2026 · Prepared for Guru Sweet Mart, Mysuru

Once Phase 1 is live, running it costs an estimated ₹5,400–₹7,600 a month (₹64,000–₹91,500 a year) in platform and gateway fees, plus Shiprocket's per-shipment courier charge if that's the aggregator chosen — typically passed through to the customer as the delivery fee, not absorbed as overhead. This excludes the one-time website build cost and is assembled from public vendor pricing; confirm current rates with each provider before quoting final numbers.
