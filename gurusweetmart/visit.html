Shipping & delivery

Domestic shipping across serviceable Indian postcodes, with delivery estimates shown before payment based on product shelf life and season.

Cancellations & refunds

Food information

Privacy & terms

We collect only what is needed for your order and communicate in your chosen language wherever possible.

What happens if I can't make it to collect my token order?

Orders not collected within the store's configured window move to a no-show or expired state per store policy; contact support for options.

Can someone else collect my order for me?

Yes, if they can show your QR code or your token number plus your verified mobile number, with staff confirmation recorded.

Is Guru Sweet Mart's food 100% vegetarian?

Yes — every product carries a clear vegetarian mark, along with ingredients and allergen information.

Which languages can I shop in?

English and Kannada are available at launch, with Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam following — more Indian languages are planned.
