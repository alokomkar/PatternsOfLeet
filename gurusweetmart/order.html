How it works

Six steps, no counter queue

  1. Open Order at Store and choose your Mysuru shop.
  2. See the current estimated preparation time before you order.
  3. Build your order and add a verified mobile number.
  4. Pay online — your order is confirmed only after verified payment.
  5. Get your token number and a signed, single-use QR code.
  6. Walk in when notified, show your QR at the counter, and collect.

Signed · Single-use · Store-bound

Verified payment first

Your order only enters the queue after payment is confirmed by the gateway — never on a client-side promise.

Single-use QR

Each QR is signed and store-bound. A second scan after handover is rejected and clearly reported.

Realistic wait times

Estimated preparation time is shown before you pay, and updates live — never a guarantee, always current.
