What this demo shows

Orders placed through the Shop → Cart → Checkout → Razorpay-placeholder flow on this device land here automatically, with the courier chosen at checkout and a packing estimate based on time of day. Changing an order's status here is also reflected on its public tracking page (Track your order) — this is how a real admin panel and customer tracking page would stay in sync once the backend exists.

Sales and delivery reports, staff logins, and role permissions are not part of this mock — they come with the backend build in the next phase.
